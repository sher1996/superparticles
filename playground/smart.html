Every dot runs the same tiny neural network and only sees its neighbours, yet together they grow the shape from one seed and heal it. Click or drag to cut holes.

How it works

Each cell holds 16 numbers: colour, opacity and 12 hidden values it uses to signal to its neighbours. One network of 8,320 weights decides how every cell updates. Nothing tells a cell where it is.
