Smart Particles 3D

A 16×16×16 voxel version (proof of concept): every voxel runs the same small network (7,776 weights), sees only its 26 neighbours, and grows a ringed planet from one seed. Drag to rotate.

This early model is most accurate around step 100–200. After that it slowly overgrows, because it was trained on short runs only (see STATUS.md). Reset to seed to start over.

Damage it to watch it heal.

Coloured point cloud of the current state, for Blender or MeshLab.
