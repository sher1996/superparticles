Draw something, then watch thousands of tiny cells learn to grow it from a single dot and heal it when it's damaged. Training runs on your own GPU in this tab, and nothing is uploaded. Every view below shows the real training, live.

3 Watch it learn

Each training step repeats the same six-part cycle. The highlighted box is what is happening right now.

Pick examplesTake 4 grids from the pool, a memory of earlier attempts.
Reset and damageThe worst becomes a fresh single seed. After step 100, one gets a hole cut in it.
Let the cells runEvery cell looks only at its 8 neighbours and updates itself, dozens of times in a row.
CompareMeasure how far the result is from your drawing: the loss.
Adjust the ruleNudge the network's 8,320 weights in the direction that lowers the loss (gradient descent).
RememberPut the results back into the pool, so later steps practise keeping and repairing the shape.

Glossary
Cell
One dot on the grid. It holds 16 numbers: red, green, blue, opacity and 12 hidden values it uses to signal to its neighbours.
Rule / network
One small neural network (8,320 weights) that every cell runs. Training only changes this rule; no cell is ever told where it is.
Seed
A grid with a single live cell in the middle. Growth always starts from here.
Pool
A memory of 256 earlier results. Re-using them teaches the rule to keep a finished shape, not just reach it once.
Damage
Cutting a random hole in a finished shape during training, so the rule learns to heal.
Loss
The average squared difference between what the cells grew and your drawing. Training tries to make it small.
Gradient descent
Working out, for every weight, which direction would lower the loss, then taking a small step that way.
Learning rate
How big those steps are. It warms up at the start and shrinks towards the end for fine-tuning.
