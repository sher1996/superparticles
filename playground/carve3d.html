The swarm is never given the 3D shape, only 2 or 3 flat pictures: front, side and top. Cameras carve it like a sculptor walking round the block.

How it works

The orchestrator watches the swarm through one camera per picture: anything a camera sees outside its outline is carved back in, the rest presses out against the walls, and gaps a camera sees are filled from depths every other view agrees on.

Tips for your own pictures

Side = seen from the right (the object's front on the left of the picture). Top = seen from above (front at the bottom). A bold silhouette on a plain or transparent background works best. Views are lined up by their outlines: side height = front height, top width = front width.
