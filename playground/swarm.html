Free-moving smart particles that assemble a heart, hold it and re-form it. Click or drag to push them apart.

How it works · research prototype

Each particle only senses neighbours within a small radius (plus its own position), and every particle runs the same network (6,336 weights), which decides its colour change and where to move.

Particles with a white ring have decided by themselves that they sit on the outline; nothing tells them where the border is. The swarm covers about 80% of the heart. The outline is crisp on the lower left and still loose on the top and right (see STATUS.md).
